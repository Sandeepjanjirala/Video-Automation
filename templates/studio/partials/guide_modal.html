❓ How to use Telugu Reel Studio

First time Make a reel YouTube video Your own voice Change things Phone Problems

1. First time only: add your keys

  1. Click ⚙ Settings & providers (bottom left; on a phone, open ☰ first).
  2. Tick Enable online services.
  3. Paste your Gemini API key (writes Telugu and picks footage) and Pixabay API key (free stock videos).
  4. Click Save settings. You never need to do this again.

2. Make an Instagram Reel (4 steps)

  1. Click + New project.
  2. Paste your script in the big box under Your script. Telugu letters or English letters, both are fine.
  3. Click 🚀 Create draft video automatically and press OK. Wait a few minutes; keep the page open. It writes the Telugu, finds a matching video for every line, adds the voice and makes a small draft.
  4. Watch the draft on Preview & export. If you like it, click 🎬 Render … for the full-quality video, then Download MP4 and upload it to Instagram.

Want to check the scenes first? Click ✂ Split into scenes instead of step 3. Each line becomes a scene and the Telugu (or English-letter captions) fill in by themselves. Fix any scene, then click Create draft video automatically.

3. Make a YouTube video (16:9, long)

  1. In a new project, set Video format to YouTube video.
  2. Paste your long script and click 🚀 Create draft video automatically, the same as a reel.
  3. A long video takes longer (a 20-minute video about an hour). Keep the computer on and the page open.

4. Use your own voice recording

Voice & visuals tab → Narration. Your recording is never changed; the video only levels its volume.

  • One file for the whole video: choose My recording, one file and upload it (MP3, M4A, WAV, AAC, OGG, OPUS or FLAC). If your scenes already have text, they are placed on the recording by the length of their text, at pauses. Listen with ▶ on each scene and drag the numbered blue lines on the waveform (or type times) until every scene starts where its words start. No script yet? Click Make scenes from pauses, then type what is said in each scene.
  • One file per scene: choose My recordings, one per scene and upload a file for each scene. Use Use from / to to cut silence or a mistake at the start or end, and Volume if one recording is quieter.
  • Captions: for a recording the caption timing is an estimate from its pauses. Open the Captions tab, play each scene and correct any caption that comes too early or late, then Save captions.
  • Recorded again? Use Replace recording. Scenes are placed again on the new file and their caption timing resets; Restore previous boundaries brings the old ones back if the new take has the same timing.

5. Change things (only if you want to)

Grey bars with ▸ open more options. Click a bar to open it, click again to close.

  • A scene's video: Voice & visuals tab → the scene → Change footage & framing. Search new stock, upload your own clip, move or zoom the picture.
  • The words: Story tab → edit the scene's Telugu or captions box.
  • Caption look: Captions tab → pick a style. Highlight key words shows important words in yellow. Turn Show captions on the video off for no captions.
  • The voice: Voice & visuals tab → Voice settings (speed, style, other voices).
  • After changing anything, click ✓ Approve revision (top right), then render again.

6. Use it on your phone

  1. On the computer: Settings → set Password for phone and other devices.
  2. Start the studio with START_PHONE_ACCESS.bat. It shows an address like http://192.168.1.20:8788.
  3. On the phone (same Wi-Fi), open that address and sign in. ☰ shows your projects; the four steps are at the bottom.

The computer does the work, so keep it on while a video is made.

7. If something goes wrong

  • A red message appears: read it; it says what to do. Fix that, then click the same button again. Finished work is reused.
  • "Gemini … quota" or "busy": the free AI limit is used up for today. Wait, or add a paid key in Settings.
  • A scene shows a title card instead of video: no good stock clip was found. Open Change footage & framing on that scene and search other words or upload a clip.
  • Render button does nothing useful: look at Ready to render? on Preview & export; it lists what is missing.
  • Delete a project: 🗑 next to it in the project list, or Delete at the top.
