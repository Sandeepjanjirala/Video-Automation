How to change a caption's words or timing

Open a scene below. Times are seconds from the start of that scene's voice (for example 1.25 or 0:01.25). Play a caption to check it, or pause the audio and set a start or end from the playhead. Saved captions replace the automatic timing for that scene. Save, then approve the new revision before rendering.
